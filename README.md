# Beyond Traditional Risk Scores

## AI-Based Early Prediction of Cardiovascular Disease Risk in Adolescents

### Overview

Cardiovascular disease (CVD) remains the leading cause of death worldwide. While current prediction models are designed primarily for adults, risk factors often begin developing during adolescence.

This research project investigates whether artificial intelligence can improve early prediction of future cardiovascular disease risk by combining physiological, lifestyle, mental health, and demographic data.

---

## Research Question

Can machine learning models predict lifetime cardiovascular disease risk in adolescents more effectively than traditional risk assessment methods?

---

## Objectives

- Conduct a comprehensive literature review.
- Identify key cardiovascular risk factors in adolescents.
- Explore the influence of mental health on cardiovascular outcomes.
- Develop and evaluate machine learning prediction models.
- Compare AI-based models with existing clinical risk scores.
- Propose future applications in preventive medicine.
