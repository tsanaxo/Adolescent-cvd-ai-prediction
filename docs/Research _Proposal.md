# AI-Based Early Prediction of Cardiovascular Disease Risk in Adolescents Using Lifestyle, Mental Health, and Physiological Factors

## Background

Cardiovascular disease (CVD) is the leading cause of death worldwide. Although heart attacks and strokes typically occur later in adulthood, many of the underlying risk factors begin developing during childhood and adolescence. Current cardiovascular risk assessment tools are primarily designed for adults, making it difficult to identify high-risk adolescents before disease progresses.

Advances in artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning offer an opportunity to analyze complex health data and identify patterns that traditional statistical methods may overlook. This project investigates whether AI can improve the early prediction of cardiovascular disease risk in adolescents by integrating lifestyle, mental health, and physiological factors into a predictive model.

---

## Research Question

Can machine learning models improve the early prediction of cardiovascular disease risk in adolescents compared with traditional cardiovascular risk assessment methods?

---

## Hypothesis

Machine learning models that incorporate physiological measurements, lifestyle behaviors, and mental health indicators will predict future cardiovascular disease risk in adolescents more accurately than traditional risk assessment methods alone.

---

## Objectives

The objectives of this research are to:

- Review current cardiovascular disease prediction models.
- Identify important cardiovascular risk factors in adolescents.
- Examine the relationship between mental health and cardiovascular health.
- Develop a machine learning model capable of predicting cardiovascular disease risk.
- Compare AI-based predictions with existing clinical approaches.
- Evaluate the strengths and limitations of AI in preventive cardiovascular medicine.

---

## Methods

This study will consist of the following stages:

1. Conduct a literature review using peer-reviewed scientific publications.
2. Obtain publicly available cardiovascular health datasets.
3. Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
4. Train and evaluate machine learning models using Python.
5. Compare model performance using established evaluation metrics.
6. Interpret findings and discuss potential clinical applications.

---

## Expected Impact

If successful, this research may demonstrate how artificial intelligence can contribute to earlier identification of cardiovascular disease risk among adolescents. Earlier detection could support preventive healthcare strategies, encourage healthier lifestyles, and inform future research into AI-assisted cardiovascular screening.

Although this project is exploratory and not intended for clinical use, it aims to contribute to ongoing research into preventive cardiology and the responsible use of artificial intelligence in medicine.
